OUR STORY

Made between precision
and instinct.

Aurelle began with one question: can luxury feel quieter and still be unforgettable?

01 / BEGINNING

We did not set out to make more perfume. We set out to make fragrance feel personal again.

01

Source

Materials are selected for texture, provenance and the emotion they carry.

02

Compose

Accords are built in layers, then edited until every note has a reason to remain.

03

Rest

Each concentrate matures for twelve weeks so the composition can settle into balance.

04

Finish

Small batches are filtered, bottled and checked by hand before leaving the atelier.

Clarity

Every material is legible. Complexity never becomes clutter.

Patience

Time is treated as an ingredient, not a production delay.

Intimacy

A fragrance should live with the wearer rather than perform over them.

AURELLE

Fragrance composed as atmosphere—precise, intimate and made to remain in memory.
